## About

I am an MBA graduate in Accounting and Information Systems from Jagannath University, and I build automation systems on n8n. Most of my work is chatbots on Telegram and Messenger, plus business workflows that connect Supabase, Groq and the Claude API.

I came into this from accounting and teaching, not from a computer science degree. That shapes how I work: I start from the business process, then decide what to automate and how to check that it runs correctly.

Two systems I run myself:

- **Career Bondhu**, a Telegram bot that gives career guidance to students and fresh graduates in Bangladesh.
- **Nepo Mart BD**, an e-commerce page where I built the Messenger sales and support automation.

I also teach Accounting, ICT and Statistics to Class 9, Class 10 and HSC students at INSAF.

Currently working on: RAG pipelines and MCP-based agent tools.
